Executive Profile

Building the commercial system around emerging technology.

For more than 25 years, I have worked where shifts in technology and consumer behavior become new advertising businesses — from the early internet and search to mobile, social, gaming and AI.

My role has been to turn that shift into a commercial system: define the proposition, build the go-to-market model, create advertiser demand, lead sales teams and strategic partnerships, connect product with ad tech and measurement, and scale repeatable revenue. I have carried quota and P&L and worked across Sales, Product, Engineering, Data, Finance, Legal and Privacy to move new offerings from concept to adoption.

Core Commercial Pillars
