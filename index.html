How I Build

From behavior shift to repeatable revenue.

The technology changes. The commercialization questions are remarkably consistent.

01

Understand the behavior

Why are people here, what changed, and what utility is creating the new behavior?

02

Find the friction

What is stopping a customer who sees the value from actually adopting the product?

03

Build the system

Connect market demand to Product, Engineering, Data, Legal, Finance, Operations and Sales.

04

Prove and repeat

Measure outcomes, learn from early customers, price intelligently and make the model repeatable.
