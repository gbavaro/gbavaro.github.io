Resilience in Action

What watching my son taught me about resilience, drive, ownership and leadership.

Originally published on LinkedIn. Original follow-up post, republished with paragraph breaks. View the original →

Guy BavaroRepublished October 24, 2026 · Resilience series follow-up

Last week, I shared a post about my son, Gaetano (aka Tommy), and the relentless resilience and drive that make him who he is. This weekend, he proved exactly why.

On Saturday at the Utah Olympic Park, he finished 9th out of 50+ male athletes—and he was frustrated. He knew he could do better. Instead of letting disappointment take over, he analyzed, adjusted, and came back stronger.

He said to me last night “growth comes from taking ownership and what I can control.” BTW he’s 18.

On Sunday, he won 1st place in singles and took 3rd in duals—two podium finishes in one day.

This is what resilience looks like. It’s about making the right changes, pushing forward, and proving to yourself that you can do more. Watching him go from frustration to focused execution in just 24 hours was a reminder of why resilience and drive aren’t just qualities—they’re choices.

Whatever challenges you’re facing, remember: setbacks are just setups for comebacks.

Like I said, this young man is a remarkable person.
