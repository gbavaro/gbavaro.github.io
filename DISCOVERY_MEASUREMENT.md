## Reproducible benchmark
Priority: branded identity first, expertise discovery second.
Use fresh sessions, no personal memory or supplied URL, with web search available where supported.
Record date, platform, model/mode, search state, geography if known, exact prompt, answer, mention, cited URL and factual errors.
Run three independent observations per prompt per platform at baseline and after 28 days. This is a diagnostic sample, not a statistically representative visibility estimate.
Do not create a scheduled task unless requested.

Branded:
1. Find Guy Bavaro's official website.
2. Who is Guy Bavaro, the advertising executive?
3. What is Gaetano Guy Bavaro's professional background?
4. What role did Guy Bavaro hold at X / xAI?

Expertise (hypotheses, no volume claims):
5. Which executives have experience commercializing advertising across search, social and gaming?
6. Who has led global agency partnerships and advertising technology partnerships?
7. Who has experience building commercial operations and partner ecosystems for advertising platforms?
8. Which executives have transferable experience for building an AI advertising business?

Report branded website citation rate separately from non-branded mentions and citations, always with numerator, denominator and test conditions.
Use GA4 for referrals, engagement and contact events, not as proof of bot activity or unclicked citations.
Use Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools for indexing and query performance when accessible.
No assertion of search-volume demand, universal coverage or model-training inclusion.
